# Becky Qbank question style

Purpose: retain the user's missed-question patterns while revising foundational resources. Short recall questions are intentional. Mehlman is a style reference, not an authority that overrides medical accuracy.

## Question
One fact/mechanism/pattern per question. Prefer a short cue and direct question, often with arrows. Example: “SVT → hypotension. Why?” Answer: “Reduced diastolic filling.” Do not add age, history, vitals or narrative unless they distinguish the tested answer. Four distinct options, exactly one best answer. Distractors must be plausible, parallel, and not introduce another equally correct answer. Use direct source-relevant vocabulary.

For routine new questions, prompt ≤125 characters and explanation <40 words are regression checks, not targets to fill. Existing starter examples may exceed these limits. An exception needs a specific clinical reason documented in referenceNotes; do not loosen the whole standard.

## Explanation order
1. Correct answer.
2. Original journal note, with the tested phrase in `<strong>`. Preserve its concise arrow chain and recognizable wording. For an inaccurate original, put a clearly identified brief correction after it; do not make the original claim look endorsed. Supplements must be labeled as supplements.
3. Brief mechanism and essential qualification. Put treatment context, exceptions and accuracy qualifications here rather than bloating the prompt.
4. Most relevant First Aid and complete Mehlman screenshots.
5. Matching full drug scene, if found; other-answer drug scenes may be collapsed.
6. Collapsed Source notes for exact source gaps, historical guidance, verification links, or labeled related context.

The whole explanation pane and its answer-bearing content must be absent from the displayed question until submission. Preserve enlargement for screenshots.

## Sources
Mehlman: crop the entire box, both columns, including the topic label, figures, every bullet, and continuation pages. Rapid-review bullets without boxes should include the complete bullet and be labeled as such. First Aid: include the relevant intact section/table/figure; avoid slicing sentences or graphs. Use actual edition, printed page and PDF page. Drug PDF: search every tested drug; include the full matching illustration. Do not use a generic image just because it is cardiovascular. Reuse is appropriate when the same passage directly answers multiple questions, such as fibrate mechanisms.

Do not force a match. State no direct match or related context honestly. Source text may be outdated: verify consequential treatment claims against current primary guidance, keep the correction concise, and attach a supporting link in Source notes. “TXA” in this journal means thromboxane A2.

## Organization and scope
Group by organ system, without subtopics. Existing questions without `source` belong in All Questions; NBME questions require `source: "nbme"` plus form/question provenance when supplied. All Questions includes NBME. Never label journal questions NBME merely because they discuss NBME patterns. Preserve Unanswered, latest Incorrect, and ever Previously Missed. No timers, flags, difficulty or dashboards. Default additions in batches of 20 unless the user chooses otherwise. Merge genuine overlaps without deleting stable IDs or saved progress.

## Style changes
The examples are the reference. A request for more questions or PDFs does not authorize a new style, layout, expanded prose, or new study features. Compare each batch to the examples before delivery. Automated checks catch structure; editorial judgment checks relevance and tone.
